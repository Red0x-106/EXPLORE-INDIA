India is home to many different cultures, landscapes, traditions and foods. Explore some interesting places, foods and facts from all 28 states.

Andhra Pradesh

Famous Place: Tirupati Temple

Tirupati is one of the most visited pilgrimage destinations in India.

Famous Food: Pulihora

Pulihora is a popular tangy rice dish made with tamarind and spices.

Interesting Fact

Tirupati is one of the world's most visited religious destinations.

Arunachal Pradesh

Famous Place: Tawang Monastery

Tawang Monastery is one of the largest Buddhist monasteries in India.

Famous Food: Thukpa

Thukpa is a warm noodle soup that is popular in the Himalayan region.

Interesting Fact

Arunachal Pradesh is the first Indian state to see the sunrise.

Assam

Famous Place: Kaziranga National Park

Kaziranga is famous for its one-horned rhinoceroses and rich wildlife.

Famous Food: Masor Tenga

Masor Tenga is a light and tangy fish curry made with sour ingredients.

Interesting Fact

Assam is famous for its tea and is one of the world's major tea-growing regions.

Bihar

Famous Place: Mahabodhi Temple

The Mahabodhi Temple in Bodh Gaya is an important Buddhist pilgrimage site.

🍛amous Food: Litti Chokha

Litti Chokha is a popular Bihari dish made with roasted wheat balls and vegetables.

Interesting Fact

Bodh Gaya is where Gautama Buddha is traditionally believed to have attained enlightenment.

Chhattisgarh

Famous Place: Chitrakote Falls

Chitrakote Falls is a wide and beautiful waterfall on the Indravati River.

Famous Food: Faraa

Faraa is a traditional Chhattisgarhi dish made mainly from rice flour.

Interesting Fact

Chitrakote Falls is often called the "Niagara Falls of India."

Goa

Famous Place: Baga Beach

Baga Beach is one of Goa's popular beaches, known for its coastline and activities.

Famous Food: Goan Fish Curry

Goan fish curry is a famous coastal dish made with fish, coconut and spices.

Interesting Fact

Goa was a Portuguese colony for around 450 years before becoming part of India in 1961.

Gujarat

Famous Place: Statue of Unity

The Statue of Unity is a huge statue dedicated to Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel.

Famous Food: Dhokla

Dhokla is a soft and savory steamed snack made from fermented batter.

Interesting Fact

The Statue of Unity is the world's tallest statue.

Haryana

Famous Place: Kurukshetra

Kurukshetra is an important historical and religious place associated with the Mahabharata.

Famous Food: Bajra Khichdi

Bajra Khichdi is a traditional dish made using pearl millet and other ingredients.

Interesting Fact

Haryana is one of India's major agricultural states and has a strong farming tradition.

Himachal Pradesh

Famous Place: Rohtang Pass

Rohtang Pass is a high mountain pass known for its snowy landscapes.

Famous Food: Siddu

Siddu is a traditional steamed bread made from wheat flour.

Interesting Fact

The name Himachal Pradesh roughly means "Land of Snowy Mountains."

Jharkhand

Famous Place: Hundru Falls

Hundru Falls is a beautiful waterfall formed by the Subarnarekha River.

Famous Food: Dhuska

Dhuska is a popular fried rice-flour snack enjoyed in Jharkhand.

Interesting Fact

Jharkhand is known for its forests and important mineral resources.

Karnataka

Famous Place: Hampi

Hampi is a UNESCO World Heritage Site known for the ruins of the Vijayanagara Empire.

Famous Food: Bisi Bele Bath

Bisi Bele Bath is a popular rice dish made with lentils, vegetables and spices.

Interesting Fact

Hampi was once the capital of the powerful Vijayanagara Empire.

Kerala

Famous Place: Alleppey Backwaters

The Alleppey backwaters are famous for their peaceful waterways and houseboats.

Famous Food: Appam

Appam is a soft rice pancake with a thin and crispy edge.

Interesting Fact

Kerala is famous for traditional houseboats called kettuvallams.

Madhya Pradesh

Famous Place: Khajuraho Temples

The Khajuraho temples are famous for their detailed stone carvings and architecture.

Famous Food: Poha

Poha is a popular breakfast made from flattened rice, vegetables and spices.

Interesting Fact

Madhya Pradesh is often called the "Heart of India" because of its central location.

Maharashtra

Famous Place: Gateway of India

The Gateway of India is a famous monument located on the waterfront of Mumbai.

Famous Food: Vada Pav

Vada Pav is a popular street food made with a potato fritter served inside a bun.

Interesting Fact

Mumbai is home to India's Hindi film industry, commonly known as Bollywood.

Manipur

Famous Place: Loktak Lake

Loktak Lake is the largest freshwater lake in northeastern India.

Famous Food: Eromba

Eromba is a traditional Manipuri dish made with vegetables and fermented fish.

Interesting Fact

Loktak Lake has floating islands called phumdis.

Meghalaya

Famous Place: Living Root Bridges

Living root bridges are natural bridges created by guiding the roots of trees.

Famous Food: Jadoh

Jadoh is a traditional Khasi rice dish made with rice and meat.

Interesting Fact

Meghalaya is famous for living root bridges made by guiding the roots of rubber trees.

Mizoram

Famous Place: Reiek

Reiek is a scenic mountain near Aizawl known for its beautiful views.

Famous Food: Bai

Bai is a traditional Mizo dish made with vegetables and other local ingredients.

Interesting Fact

Mizoram has one of the highest literacy rates among Indian states.

Nagaland

Famous Place: Kisama Heritage Village

Kisama Heritage Village showcases the traditional culture and architecture of Nagaland.

Famous Food: Smoked Pork

Smoked pork is a popular traditional food in Nagaland.

Interesting Fact

Nagaland is home to many Naga tribes with their own distinct traditions and cultures.

Odisha

Famous Place: Konark Sun Temple

The Konark Sun Temple is a famous historic temple designed like a giant stone chariot.

Famous Food: Dalma

Dalma is a traditional dish made with lentils, vegetables and spices.

Interesting Fact

The Konark Sun Temple is designed as a giant stone chariot with beautifully carved wheels.

Punjab

Famous Place: Golden Temple

The Golden Temple in Amritsar is one of the most important Sikh pilgrimage sites.

Famous Food: Amritsari Kulcha

Amritsari Kulcha is a stuffed and crispy flatbread popular in Punjab.

Interesting Fact

The Golden Temple has four entrances, symbolizing that people from all directions are welcome.

Rajasthan

Famous Place: Hawa Mahal

Hawa Mahal is a famous pink-colored palace in Jaipur with many small windows.

Famous Food: Dal Baati Churma

Dal Baati Churma is a traditional Rajasthani meal made with lentils, baked wheat balls and sweet churma.

Interesting Fact

Rajasthan is home to the Thar Desert, one of the major deserts of India.

Sikkim

Famous Place: Tsomgo Lake

Tsomgo Lake is a beautiful high-altitude lake surrounded by mountains.

Famous Food: Momos

Momos are steamed dumplings that are very popular across Sikkim.

Interesting Fact

Sikkim became India's 22nd state in 1975.

Tamil Nadu

Famous Place: Meenakshi Amman Temple

The Meenakshi Amman Temple is famous for its colorful towers and detailed architecture.

Famous Food: Idli

Idli is a soft steamed food made from fermented rice and lentil batter.

Interesting Fact

Tamil Nadu is home to some of India's oldest surviving temple architecture and traditions.

Telangana

Famous Place: Charminar

Charminar is one of Hyderabad's most famous historical monuments.

Famous Food: Hyderabadi Biryani

Hyderabadi biryani is a famous rice dish made with spices, rice and meat or vegetables.

Interesting Fact

Hyderabad is famous for the Charminar and its popular style of biryani.

Tripura

Famous Place: Ujjayanta Palace

Ujjayanta Palace is a historic palace located in Agartala.

Famous Food: Mui Borok

Mui Borok refers to the traditional cuisine of the indigenous people of Tripura.

Interesting Fact

Tripura has a long tradition of bamboo and cane handicrafts.

Uttar Pradesh

Famous Place: Taj Mahal

The Taj Mahal is a world-famous monument built in Agra and known for its white marble architecture.

Famous Food: Awadhi Biryani

Awadhi biryani is a flavorful rice dish associated with the Awadhi cuisine of Uttar Pradesh.

Interesting Fact

The Taj Mahal can appear slightly different in color depending on the light and time of day.

Uttarakhand

Famous Place: Valley of Flowers

The Valley of Flowers is famous for its colorful alpine flowers and mountain scenery.

Famous Food: Aloo Ke Gutke

Aloo Ke Gutke is a traditional potato dish cooked with local spices.

Interesting Fact

Uttarakhand is often called "Devbhoomi," which means "Land of the Gods."

West Bengal

Famous Place: Victoria Memorial

Victoria Memorial is a famous marble monument and museum in Kolkata.

Famous Food: Kolkata Biryani

Kolkata biryani is known for its fragrant rice, potato and flavorful spices.

Interesting Fact

Kolkata was the capital of British India until 1911.
